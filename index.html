About Blueprint Home Care

A note from our founder.

Blueprint Home Care was founded by Andrea Parado, a certified project manager who spent years helping busy families run their homes with less stress. After seeing how hard it is to find home care you can truly trust, she built Blueprint on a simple idea: bring executive-level organization and genuine warmth to caring for the people we love right here in Morris County.
