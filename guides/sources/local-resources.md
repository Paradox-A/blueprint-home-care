# Reference: third-party local/national resources for Approach A roundups

Internal reference file for the weekly resource-guide routine. Not published on the site — used to seed "curated local resource roundup" guides. Always verify each URL is still live via WebFetch before citing it in a published guide; do not assume anything here is still current.

## Morris County, NJ — Office on Aging, Disabilities and Community Programming
- Homepage: https://www.morrishumanservices.org
- Phone: (973) 285-6848 or (800) 564-4656
- Publishes a "Caregiver Resource Directory" and an "Adult Day Care Facilities" list for Morris County. The versions Blueprint has on file are dated 2021-2022 and may be stale — check the live site for a current edition before citing specifics; link to the homepage/relevant section rather than a specific old PDF unless you've confirmed a current one exists at a stable URL.
- Note printed on their Adult Day Care list: "This publication is not an endorsement" — Blueprint's roundup should carry the same framing (informational listing, not an endorsement).

## NJ Aging & Disability Resource Connection (ADRC)
- Phone: 1-877-222-3737
- Statewide single point of entry for aging/disability services, benefits screening (including JACC and Statewide Respite intake), and referrals. Good as a general "start here" resource regardless of county.

## Aging Life Care Association (ALCA)
- Homepage: https://www.aginglifecare.org
- National nonprofit professional association for geriatric/aging life care managers ("the experts in aging well"). Publishes consumer-facing guidance. Two white papers Blueprint has on file (used for background/theme inspiration only — summarize the ideas in original words, do not republish their text):
  - "Agency or Independent: Considerations in Hiring" — Bunni Dybnis, MA, LMFT, CMC (Aug 2017)
  - "The Six Hidden Costs to Caring for an Aging Parent" — Jullie Gray, MSW, LICSW, CMC (May 2015)
  - Both are dated (2015/2017) — verify ALCA's site for current versions/URLs rather than assuming these old copies are still hosted at their original location.
- ALCA's "Find an Aging Life Care Expert" directory (check aginglifecare.org for the current path) is a legitimate outbound link for readers whose situation calls for a professional geriatric care manager rather than (or alongside) a home care agency.
